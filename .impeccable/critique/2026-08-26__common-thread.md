## Verdict

The Common Field is now recognizably IssuePages: a shared indigo repository
field, chalk reading folios, oxblood issue joins, and source notation that stays
attached to the writing. The visual metaphor supports the product mechanism
without becoming decorative craft imagery.

Responsive inspection at 390, 768, and 1440 pixels found no clipped controls,
horizontal page overflow, unreadable metadata, or broken content order across
the homepage, article, universal reader, search, embed builder, and embedded
publication. The minimal embed remains host-neutral.

## Remaining low-priority opportunities

- A future usability pass can test whether first-time visitors distinguish the
  moderated publishing repository from the universal read-only reader without
  reading the supporting paragraph.
- Very long repositories make compact mobile embeds tall by design; filtering
  and pagination, rather than denser type, are the appropriate escape hatches.
